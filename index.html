Prompts follow the Pearson Edexcel International GCSE specifications for Physics (4PH1), Chemistry (4CH1) and Biology (4BI1). Every prompt shows the spec point it comes from, and Paper 2 marks content shown in bold in the spec (Paper 2 only). Correct answers from beyond the spec count too, and they usually sink deepest. Rarity is our own estimate of how often students give each answer, until enough people play to measure it. Pearson International GCSE specs.
